TicBird: Tiko the Courier

One tap. Fly the parcels home. · Türkçe

Meet Tiko, a little teal courier bird with a bag full of parcels. Tap anywhere to flap and glide between cargo crates hanging from cranes and stacked on the docks. Every gap you clear is a parcel delivered.

Four worlds

Harbor Bazaar, Cloud Gardens, Neon Night and Storm Cape, each with its own twist and music.

Four ways to fly

Classic Route, a fresh Daily Route every day, Zen Flight with no game over, and 20 Courier Jobs.

Made for everyone

Reduce Motion, High Contrast, Assist Mode, VoiceOver and Dynamic Type.

Free, and fully playable offline. Never an ad mid-flight, and a one-time "Ad-free" purchase removes all ads and keeps the rewards. Your progress is kept in your own iCloud account.

On the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
