Will my progress move to a new phone?

Yes. With iCloud on and the same Apple Account, your progress syncs on its own. The first sync can take a few minutes; closing and reopening the game once also helps.

My Ad-free purchase is missing

Tap Settings › Ads › Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

When do ads appear?

Never while you fly. Ads between runs start after your first 10 runs, appear only when you tap a button on the receipt after a run, and are spaced out (at least 3 minutes and 3 runs apart). Double-coin and Second chance ads play only when you choose them.

How do I change my ad consent?

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland: Settings › Privacy options in the game. For tracking permission, use your iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Can I play offline?

Yes, completely. Without internet, ads are skipped silently; no part of the game depends on a connection.

The game feels too hard

Turn on Settings › Assist Mode: the game runs at 80% speed, gaps get wider and your best score is kept separately. In Zen Flight you can't lose at all.
