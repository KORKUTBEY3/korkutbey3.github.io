TicBird Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · Türkçe

TicBird is a free game starring Tiko, a courier bird. The game itself plays fully offline; only ads connect to the internet. This policy explains what data goes where. The same text can be read offline in the game under Settings › Privacy policy.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store or share your name, email address or game data. No account is needed.

Ads (Google AdMob)

On iPhone and iPad, ads are served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, information about the ads you see and how you interact with the app, and crash and performance data. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your consent

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears on first launch; you can change your choice later in Settings › Privacy options. Your advertising identifier is used only if you allow it in Apple's "Allow Tracking" prompt; if you don't, ads are shown without personalisation and the game plays exactly the same.

Ad-free

The one-time "Ad-free" purchase permanently removes all ads; double coins and Second chance rewards come without ads. Apple processes the payment; your payment details never reach us. Without Ad-free, rewarded ads play only when you choose them.

Your progress

Your scores, coins, costumes and other progress are stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud account, so they survive a device change. We cannot access this data. Your settings stay on your device only.

Children

TicBird is not directed at children but is suitable for all ages; ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and the date above will be revised.
